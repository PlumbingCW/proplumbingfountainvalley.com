Privacy Policy

Information we receive

When you call (714) 750-8637, we receive your phone number, the time of the call and anything you tell us about your plumbing problem and address. This website does not have forms, accounts or comment sections, so it does not ask you to type personal details.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the person who answers at the start of the call.

How we use it

We use call information to dispatch a plumber, confirm your appointment, improve call handling and resolve questions about a visit. We do not sell your personal information.

Website analytics and cookies

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and an embedded Google Map, and those providers may receive your IP address and browser details when the page loads. We do not run advertising pixels on this site.

Email

If you email contact@proplumbingfountainvalley.com, we use your message and address only to reply to you.

Your choices

To ask what information we hold about a call or to request deletion where the law allows, email contact@proplumbingfountainvalley.com with the phone number and approximate date of the call.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date below when we do. Last updated November 10, 2026.
